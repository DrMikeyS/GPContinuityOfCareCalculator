Welcome to the GP Continuity Calculator

This tool analyses appointment data to calculate the Usual Provider of Care (UPC) index for your practice.
To get started: Export appointment search results from your clinical system (EMIS or SystmOne) as a CSV file, then upload it below.

  • EMIS: Use the "Appointments" search and export results as CSV.
  • SystmOne: Use the "Appointments" reporting tool and export as CSV.

Upload Appointment CSV


            


            

              

                

                  

                    
                  

                  

                    

                      

                        UPC is calculated as the maximum number of appointments with a single GP (i.e. the GP the patient has seen most commonly) divided by the total number of appointments for that patient.
                      

                      
    
                        
  • UPC 0.7 or higher: Good continuity
    • 
                        
  • UPC 0.4 to 0.7: Some continuity, with room for improvement
    • 
                        
  • UPC below 0.4: Poor continuity
