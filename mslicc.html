Welcome to the mSLICC Continuity Calculator

This tool analyses appointment data to calculate the Modified St Leonard's Index of Continuity of Care (mSLICC) for your practice.
To get started: Export appointment search results from your clinical system (EMIS or SystmOne) as a CSV file, then upload it below.

  • EMIS: Use the "Appointments" search and export results as CSV.
  • SystmOne: Use the "Appointments" reporting tool and export as CSV.

                        mSLICC (Modified St Leonard’s Index of Continuity of Care) is a monthly measure of GP continuity representing the percentage of consultations in which patients see their own regular GP.
                      

                      

                        Equation (in words):

                        mSLICC = (Number of appointments in the measurement month where the patient was seen by their regular GP) ÷ (Total number of appointments in the measurement month for patients who have a regular GP) × 100.
                      

                    

                  

                

                

                  

                    
                  

                  

                    

                      
    
                        
  1. Use all appointment data immediately prior to the measurement month (exclude the current month).
    2. 
                        
  2. Identify all GPs that patient has seen in that appointment data period.
    3. 
                        
  3. Count the number of appointments with each GP.
    4. 
                        
  4. Select as the regular GP the one the patient has seen at least twice and most frequently.
    5. 
                        
  5. If there is a tie (two or more GPs with the same highest count), choose the GP with the most recent appointment with that patient.
    6. 
                        
  6. If no GP has been seen at least twice, the patient has no regular GP and is excluded from the denominator.
    7. 
                      

                    

                  

                

                

                  

                    
                  

                  

                    

                      

                        

                          
                            

                              Variable
                              Meaning
                            

                          
                          
                            

                              month_start, month_end
                              Start and end dates of the measurement month.
                            

                            

                              appointments
                              All GP appointment records (fields: patient_id, gp_id, datetime).
                            

                            

                              lookback_start
                              month_start minus start of appointment data period.
                            

                            

                              lookback_end
                              month_start minus 1 day.
                            

                            

                              regular_gp[patient_id]
                              GP determined using the lookback rules above.
                            

                            

                              month_appointments
                              All GP appointments where datetime ∈ [month_start, month_end] and patient_id belongs to patients with a regular GP.
                            

                            

                              appointments_with_regular_gp
                              Subset of month_appointments where gp_id == regular_gp[patient_id].
                            

                          
                        

                      

                      

                        Formula (in pseudocode):

                        mSLICC = (len(appointments_with_regular_gp) / len(month_appointments)) * 100
                      

                    

                  

                

              

            


            

            

              
            

            

              Scores in the first three months may appear artificially high because patients have not yet had time to establish a regular GP within the dataset.
